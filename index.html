How it works

From a plain-language request to a result a person can sign.

  1. Ask in plain languageThe agent asks you when it is unsure, and uses the documents and records in your workspace.
  2. The agent does the jobIt works on your own hardware, so your data stays inside your environment.
  3. Code checks every claimEach quoted passage and cited record entry is checked against your own files. A claim that cannot be found is marked or removed, not believed.
  4. A person decidesDoubtful findings are held for a human. Every step is logged, and any result can be re-checked independently.
What you can rely on

Built so the answer is checkable, not just plausible.

These are properties of the platform design. Where we have measured them, it has been on synthetic test data, and we say so below.

Sources shown

Every answer points to the passage or record it rests on, so a reviewer can look at the evidence directly.

Claims checked by code

Verification is ordinary code, not another opinion from the model. Unsupported claims are held back or removed.

A log that shows edits

Every step is written to a log that cannot be changed without it showing, and a result can be replayed and re-checked.

Your hardware, your data

The on-site configuration makes no outbound calls. Hosted models are an explicit opt-in, off by default.

Separate workspaces

Each job keeps its own documents, records and people apart, so one team's work does not leak into another's.

A person is accountable

Results wait for sign-off by a named reviewer. The platform proposes; people decide.

The first agent

Reviewing who accessed which patient records.

The first agent we have built and tested reviews access to patient records against a hospital's own privacy policies. It produces an evidence report that an examiner could read: each finding, the policy text it rests on, the log events behind it, what was checked and what was not, and who signed off.

The same platform is meant for other jobs as you need them, and each agent earns trust separately.

We offer a six-week paid trial on a hospital's de-identified data, delivered with a partner firm where possible. You see measured results on your own cases, including where it falls short, before you commit to anything more.
