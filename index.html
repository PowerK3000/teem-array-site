How it works

  1. Ask in plain language. The agent asks you when it is unsure and uses the documents and records in your workspace.
  2. The agent does the job. It works on your own hardware, so your data stays inside your environment.
  3. Code checks every claim. Each quoted passage and each cited record entry is checked against your own files. A claim that cannot be found is marked or removed, not believed.
  4. A person decides. Doubtful findings are held for a human, every step is written to a log that cannot be edited without it showing, and any result can be re-checked independently.

What we have built first

The first agent reviews access to patient records against a hospital's own privacy policies and produces an evidence report that an examiner could read. Separate workspaces keep each job's documents, data and people apart, and more agents can be set up as new jobs need them.

We offer a six-week paid trial on a hospital's de-identified data, delivered with a partner firm where possible. You see measured results on your own cases, including where it falls short, before you commit to anything more.

What we do not claim yet
